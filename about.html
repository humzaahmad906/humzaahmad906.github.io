About

Hi, I'm Humza.

Senior Machine Learning Engineer at PackageX. I ship production ML systems — across cloud, on-prem, and edge.

I'm an ML engineer focused on shipping. Five years in, I've built production systems end-to-end — from data collection and training through quantization, deployment, and SDK packaging — across cloud, on-prem, and edge.

I'm currently Senior Machine Learning Engineer at PackageX in Islamabad, where I lead an on-premise real-time document processing pipeline for conveyor-belt logistics. The work spans line-scan camera tracking, a custom OCR engine, LayoutLMv3 entity extraction, and a compact VLM track (Qwen3.5-VL fine-tuned with SFT → DPO → QAT) deployed 4-bit quantized on iOS via MLX Swift. We hit 94% F1 on in-house data — vs 65% for GPT-4o off the shelf.

Before PackageX I worked at Cipher Savvy and RevolveAI on NLP and LLM systems: tweet generation, web-scale document search with Pinecone, multi-camera person identification, BERT/Roberta classifiers on AWS, and U-Net medical imaging. I hold a BS in Electrical Engineering from NUST.
